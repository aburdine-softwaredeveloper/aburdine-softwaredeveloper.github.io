01 — About

Engineer with a broader perspective

Hi, I'm Austin — a software developer with 4+ years of professional experience building and maintaining applications in enterprise environments. My technical work spans Visual Basic, C#, C++, CSS, and Bootstrap, with development managed through Azure DevOps and Git-based workflows.

What sets me apart is an uncommon academic background: I hold two bachelor's degrees — one in Social Work from Evangel University and one in Software Development from Western Governors University. That combination gives me a genuine understanding of the people who use the software I build, not just the systems underneath.

I believe good software is built with empathy for its users, discipline in its craft, and a team that communicates well. I'm looking to bring that mindset to a team doing meaningful work.
